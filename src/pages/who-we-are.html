Who We Are

Maritime Expertise. Local Knowledge. Global Reach.

GF Maritime is a Panama-based ship agency providing comprehensive agency and maritime support to vessels transiting the Panama Canal and calling at Panamanian ports.

Our focus is simple: to make vessel operations in Panama as efficient, well-coordinated and seamless as possible.

From canal transit arrangements and port agency to husbandry, crew logistics, bunker coordination and specialist marine support, GF Maritime provides a single point of contact for the services vessels require throughout their time in Panama.

Built on Proven Maritime Experience

GF Maritime is a part of wider international network covering Singapore, Middle East, Turkey, Spain, Netherlands, Belgium and US Gulf, bringing together their extensive and complementary expertise and strengths in ship agency and international maritime services.

Together, these capabilities provide GF Maritime with a strong foundation from which to deliver integrated agency and maritime services in Panama and support the future expansion of our services across the Americas.

Vision

To be Panama’s trusted maritime partner of choice.To set the standard for professional, responsive and integrated ship agency services in Panama, while building a platform for sustainable growth across the Americas.

Mission

Our Approach

One Team. One Point of Contact. Complete Support.
