Website Cookie Policy

October 2026

At GF Maritime, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our website, analyse site performance and support our services. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone or tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device and store certain information, such as user preferences or login details.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: To enable website functionality, such as navigating the site and accessing secure areas.
  • Performance Cookies: To collect anonymised data about how users interact with our website, helping us improve its design and functionality.
  • Functional Cookies: To remember your preferences, such as language selection, for a more personalised experience.
  • Analytics and Tracking Cookies: To understand user behaviour and improve our services through tools like Google Analytics.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that expire once you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, please note that disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of our website.

For more information on managing cookies, visit All About Cookies or consult your browser’s help section.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party cookies provided by trusted partners, such as analytics providers, to understand and improve the performance of our website. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies.

Consent to Use Cookies

By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by changing your browser settings or contacting us.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology or legislation. The most recent version will always be available on this page.
