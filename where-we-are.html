    Where we are

    Strategically Positioned in Panama

    Located in Panama City, GF Maritime is ideally positioned to support vessels transiting the Panama Canal and calling at Panamanian ports.

    Our location provides convenient access to Panama’s maritime network and enables our team to coordinate agency services across both the Canal and the country’s Atlantic and Pacific operations.

    Our Coverage

    Ports & Terminals Across Panama

    GF Maritime provides agency and maritime support across key ports and terminals throughout Panama, including:

    Atlantic / Caribbean Coast

    • Cristobal Terminal
    • SSA Marine Terminal (MIT)
    • Colon Container Terminal (CCT)
    • All other main ports in the Atlantic

    Pacific Coast

    • Balboa Terminal
    • PSA Panama International Terminal
    • Petroterminal de Panama (Charco Azul/Chiriqui Grande)
    • All other main ports in the Pacific

    Panama Canal

    • Atlantic anchorages – Limon Bay (Cristobal)
    • Pacific anchorages – Balboa / Flamenco
    • Gatun Lake anchorage (in-transit: if approved)

    Panama’s Maritime Gateway

    Connecting Two Oceans.
    Supporting Global Trade.

    At the heart of global maritime trade, the Panama Canal connects the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans and provides a critical route for vessels moving between major international markets.

    GF Maritime is strategically positioned to support vessels throughout their Panama Canal transit and port call requirements, with in-depth local knowledge and operational expertise across Panama’s maritime environment.

    Wherever your vessel needs to be in Panama, GF Maritime is ready to support the journey.
