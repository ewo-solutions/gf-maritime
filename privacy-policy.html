    Website Privacy Policy

    Revision 00 · October 2026

    GF Maritime (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or engage with our services. By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

    1. Data Collection

    1.1 Types of Data Collected

    We may collect the following types of personal data:

    • Name, contact details (email address, phone number, address).
    • Job history, qualifications and other information submitted through job applications.
    • Company details (for business inquiries).
    • Browsing information (e.g., IP address, browser type, and operating system).
    • Preferences and interests provided through online forms.

    1.2 Methods of Data Collection

    Personal data may be collected through:

    • Online forms (e.g., contact forms, job application submissions).
    • Cookies and tracking technologies.
    • Direct communication (e.g., email, phone).
    • Analytics tools to monitor website usage.

    2. Data Usage

    2.1 Purpose of Data Collection

    We collect and use your data for the following purposes:

    • To respond to inquiries and provide requested information.
    • To process job applications and manage recruitment activities.
    • To improve our website’s functionality and user experience.
    • To comply with legal obligations.

    2.2 Data Access

    Your data will only be accessed by authorized personnel, including:

    • GF Maritime employees and contractors.
    • Third-party service providers who assist us in delivering our services (e.g., IT support, recruitment platforms).

    3. Data Storage and Security

    3.1 Data Storage

    • Personal and all data is stored in encrypted cloud services for backup and operational purposes.

    3.2 Security Measures

    We employ robust security measures to protect your data, including:

    • Encryption of sensitive information.
    • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
    • Access controls to limit data access to authorized personnel only.

    4. Data Sharing

    4.1 Third-Party Sharing

    Your data may be shared with third parties under the following conditions:

    • To comply with legal or regulatory requirements.
    • To trusted service providers who perform functions on our behalf, such as hosting or recruitment services.

    4.2 Assurance

    We do not sell your personal data or use it for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

    5. Data Retention

    5.1 Retention Period

    Personal data will be retained for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law.

    5.2 Data Deletion

    Upon request, we will delete your personal data, provided it is no longer required for legal or operational purposes.

    6. Data Subject Rights

    You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Access: Request access to the data we hold about you.
    • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations.
    • Objection: Object to data processing in certain circumstances.
    • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization.

    To exercise your rights, contact us at panama@gf-maritime.com.

    7. Consent

    7.1 Obtaining Consent

    • Consent is obtained when you provide personal data through our website (e.g., submitting a form).
    • By using the website, you consent to the use of cookies and tracking technologies.

    7.2 Withdrawing Consent

    You can withdraw consent for data processing at any time by contacting us at panama@gf-maritime.com.

    8. Cookies and Tracking

    8.1 Use of Cookies

    Our website uses cookies to:

    • Enhance functionality and improve your browsing experience.
    • Analyse website usage through tools such as Google Analytics.
    • Enable specific features, such as remembering preferences.

    8.2 Managing Cookies

    • You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.
    • For more information about cookies, visit All About Cookies or consult your browser’s help section.

    9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date will be updated accordingly. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your data.

    10. Contact Us

    If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

    panama@gf-maritime.com
